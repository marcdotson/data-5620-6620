# Regression Discontinuity


- Another quasi-experimental design where we compare units just above
  and below a cutoff that determines the treatment
- The **running variable** is the variable that determines whether a
  unit receives the treatment or not
- The **cutoff** is the threshold on the running variable that
  determines treatment assignment
- The **bandwidth** is the range around the cutoff that we use to
  compare treated and control units
- The key assumption is that units just above and below the cutoff are
  similar in all respects except for the treatment assignment, a kind of
  matching procedure within the bandwidth
- … (*The Effect* p. 300)
